How it works

Three steps, no templates to set up.

  1. 01

    Snap or upload

    Photos from your phone, scanned PDFs, a whole folder at once. Crooked, folded and coffee-stained pages are fine.

  2. 02

    Mực reads every line

    It finds dates, tax codes, items and totals, fixes "70k" and "70.000đ" into one number, and checks that the lines add up to the total.

  3. 03

    Review, then export

    Anything uncertain is highlighted for a ten-second look. Export to Excel, Google Sheets or CSV.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Can it read messy handwriting?

Most of it. When Mực is less than confident about a value it marks it for you to check instead of guessing.

Where are my files stored?

Files are processed and deleted after 24 hours. Exports stay in your own Excel or Google account.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Anything that imports Excel or CSV works. The Team plan adds an API for direct integration.

What if a total does not match?

The row is flagged in red with the expected and found totals, so you can fix it in seconds.
